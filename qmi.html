Questionnaire sur l'imagerie mentale de Betts, forme abrégée de Sheehan (QMI)

This questionnaire measures the clarity and vividness of your ability of mental imagery. You will be asked to generate an image of specific items in your mind, and to rate how clear and vivid you "see" these items in your mind.

Example: Think of a red traffic light. Use the following scale to describe how clear and vivid you "see" the red traffic light: 1 = Very vivid and clear as in reality ; 2 = Vivid and almost as clear as in reality ; 3 = generally clear and vivid ; 4 = not so clear and vivid but still recognizable ; 5 = vague and unclear ; 6 = very vague and hardly recognizable ; 7 = I think of it, but do not have an image before me.

Please do the same for each of the following items. There are no correct or false answers and there is no time limit. Do not linger too long with a specific item; a first impression is often the right one. Please do not skip any items.

Items en anglais (version diffusée par J. F. Kihlstrom, Université de Californie à Berkeley) : version française non retrouvée. Droits : « Licence requise : incertaine ».

35 questions 7 modalités sensorielles score de 35 à 245

Résultat

Cotation additive des réponses (1 à 7) : un score bas signifie une imagerie plus vive. La page source ne détaille pas de seuils ; aucun étalonnage n’est repris ici.

Sheehan, P. W. (1967). A shortened form of Betts’ Questionnaire upon Mental Imagery. Journal of Clinical Psychology, 23, 386-389 ; d’après Betts, G. H. (1909). Outil d’évaluation, ne remplace pas une évaluation clinique.
