ISEL-40 : Interpersonal Support Evaluation List (40 items)

40 énoncés en anglais, non traduits, sur la disponibilité perçue du soutien social. Réponse en 4 points, de « definitely false » à « definitely true ».

INSTRUCTIONS: This scale is made up of a list of statements each of which may or may not be true about you. For each statement check "definitely true" if you are sure it is true about you and "probably true" if you think it is true but are not absolutely certain. Similarly, you should check "definitely false" if you are sure the statement is false and "probably false" is you think it is false but are not absolutely certain.

Questions en anglais, non traduites (aucune version française lue). Licence requise : incertaine.

40 questions 8 minutes definitely false → definitely true

Résultat

Cohen & Hoberman (1983) ; Cohen et al. (1985). Outil d'auto-évaluation : ne remplace pas un entretien clinique ni un bilan.
