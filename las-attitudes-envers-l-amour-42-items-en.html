LAS : Love Attitudes Scale (42 items)

42 énoncés en anglais, non traduits, sur vos attitudes envers l'amour : six styles d'amour. Réponse de « strongly agree » à « strongly disagree ».

Some of the items refer to a specific love relationship, while others refer to general attitudes and beliefs about love. Whenever possible, answer the questions with your current partner in mind. If you are not currently dating anyone, answer the questions with your most recent partner in mind. If you have never been in love, answer in terms of what you think your responses would most likely be.

Questions en anglais, non traduites (aucune version française lue). Licence requise : incertaine.

42 questions 8 minutes strongly agree → strongly disagree

Résultat

Hendrick & Hendrick (1986) ; Hendrick, Hendrick & Dicke (1998). Outil d'auto-évaluation : ne remplace pas un entretien clinique ni un bilan.
