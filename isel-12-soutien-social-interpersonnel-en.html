ISEL-12 : Interpersonal Support Evaluation List (12 items)

12 énoncés en anglais, non traduits, sur la disponibilité perçue de personnes pour vous aider ou vous accompagner. Réponse en 4 points, de « definitely false » à « definitely true ».

This scale is made up of a list of statements each of which may or may not be true about you. For each statement circle "definitely true" if you are sure it is true about you and "probably true" if you think it is true but are not absolutely certain. Similarly, you should circle "definitely false" if you are sure the statement is false and "probably false" if you think it is false but are not absolutely certain.

Questions en anglais, non traduites (aucune version française lue). Licence requise : incertaine.
